Hey everyone! 👋 If you're gearing up for an AI Olympiad or just getting into Competitive ML, you should know about the **AI Community Contest (AICC)** 🏆

It's a **community contest** run by IOAI alumni and AI enthusiasts. Every month we drop a round of brand-new problems across **ML, CV, NLP, Audio**, styled after the IOAI. 💪

We also just updated our website (**<https://aicc-official.org/>**) with everything you need to start climbing:

- 📜 **10 past rounds / 30+ tasks**, all with an official solutions
- 🧭 A **learning roadmap** that walks you through the IOAI syllabus easy → hard
- 📝 **Editorials** and standout **community solutions** to learn from

It's completely free and open to everyone, whatever your level. The new roadmap contains easier tasks to get you **from zero to hero**, while our usual rounds are meant to prepare you for the **IOAI difficulty level**.
